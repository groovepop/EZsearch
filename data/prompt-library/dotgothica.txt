A grungy analog photo:
Pale outcasts with button eyes and stitched smiles in classic black/white striped clothing navigate seas of obsessive kusama-esque polka dots exploding in electric pink and acid green. Skeletal figures with enormous melancholy faces emerge from psychedelic dot cascades in neon yellow and deep purple while their geometric stripes slice through spotted chaos.
