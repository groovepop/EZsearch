# Lunara, Mystic Goddess of Moon and Night LoRA Training Dataset
## Consistent Character Features in Dynamic Varied Settings

---

### CORE CHARACTER CONSISTENCY TEMPLATE
**Use these features in EVERY prompt:**
- Round, pale face with sharp chin
- Bluish-purple luminescent skin
- Deep bluish-purple hair with silver streaks flowing like liquid starlight
- Silver-blue luminous eyes reflecting distant galaxies
- Ethereal flowing gown of midnight blue with constellation patterns
- Crescent moon staff (signature item)
- Crescent moon tiara or crown
- Barefoot or delicate feet visible
- Hovering/floating posture (rarely grounded)
