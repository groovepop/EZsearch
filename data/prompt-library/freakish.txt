Prompt: Native American dreamcatcher with Seussian feathers and striped beads expands into room-sized web. Sleeping figure tangles in threads, body becoming part of weave. Dreams leak out as paint drops in acid colors. Feathers grow into wings trying to fly away. Center hole shows swirling void of collected nightmares. Spider with human face weaves dreamer into design. Bedroom walls dissolve into web pattern extending infinitely. dreamer entrapment, reverse catching, web absorption.
