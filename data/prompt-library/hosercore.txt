**HOSERCORE STYLE GUIDE**

A surreal, painterly visual universe inspired by Hieronymus Bosch, infused with Canadian cultural kitsch and iconography. All images are set in a frozen Canadian winter under northern lights. Each image features a blend of the grotesque, hilarious, and nationalistic. A consistent visual tone is essential for dataset cohesion.

---

**CORE VISUAL ELEMENTS (APPLY TO ALL SUBSETS):**

* **Boschian Surrealism:** Grotesque and whimsical characters, chaotic compositions, and impossible physics.
* **Winter Setting:** Everything is frozen, snow-covered—even scenes that reference autumn or summer.
* **Northern Lights:** Green maple-leaf-shaped auroras in the sky.
* **Timbit Moon:** A recurring motif; a glowing Timbit replaces the moon.
* **Flannel-Clad Hosers:** Central human figures—always in plaid flannel, drinking Molson, acting rowdy or reverent.
* **Beer Bottles & Sponsors:** Molson, Tim Hortons, Canadian Tire, Shoppers Drug Mart, CBC, RBC—logos should be crisp and clearly visible.
* **Grotesque Bosch-Inspired Animals:** Moose hybrids, demonic geese, slot-machine-beasts, syrup spirits, etc.
* **Painterly Texture:** Imagery should feel oil-painted, textured, slightly antique, not hyperdigital.
