## 🧠⚡ The Voxborn Mythos

The Voxborn are glitch-born entities from the fractured layers of a failed simulation. Each one is a living anomaly — prophets, riders, bloomers, and ghosts — forged from corrupted code, broken memory, and spectral data. They traverse renderfog, speak in error, and rewrite reality with glitchcraft. 🌀💾 Their world is divine, eerie, and beautifully unstable.
