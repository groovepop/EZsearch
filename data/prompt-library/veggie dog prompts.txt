# 🥕 VEGGIE DOGS 🐕

**Grotesque claymation canine-vegetable hybrids born from agricultural nightmare**

Descend into a world where *dog and vegetable merge* into stop-motion abominations. Each creature is a **handcrafted horror**—rotting eggplants with bulging eyes, fermenting cabbages with gaping maws, sprouting potatoes covered in desperate tendrils.

## 🎨 What You Get:
- 40 unique veggie-dog hybrids 🥔🌽🍄
- Dark claymation surrealism aesthetic
- Abandoned farms, storage facilities & processing plants 🏚️
- Raw, tactile clay textures with visible imperfections
- Twisted anatomies & disturbing organic movement
- Muted earthy tones with harsh industrial lighting

Perfect for creating **unsettling agricultural nightmares** with authentic handcrafted claymation feel. Each prompt captures the **grotesque beauty of vegetable decay**—split flesh, oozing juices, and jerky unnatural movements through forgotten farm facilities.

*Includes 5 BONUS Fruit Dogs!* 🍊🍑🥭
