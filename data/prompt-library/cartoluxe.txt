📍PHASE I — Playful Awakening (1–10)

(curious, light, childlike-but-smart)


1 — The First Root

A city that grows like a garden and a garden that remembers it was once a city.
A thin black line stretches across the landscape like a curious root. A few simple buildings sprout tiny leaves, as if trying the idea of growth for the first time. Spirals hover above them like amused thoughts. The sky holds three dots: blue, red, yellow — watching.
Inspired by Joan Miró: playful abstraction, floating symbolic forms, primary color accents.

2 — The Sprouting Street

A city that grows like a garden and a garden that remembers it was once a city.

**Cartoluxe** is a surreal Miró-inspired world where the border between landscape and architecture gently dissolves. Shapes behave like language, cities bloom like gardens, and meaning feels whispered rather than spoken. This model generates floating symbolic forms, playful thin lines, wandering dots, ladders, seeds, spirals, and poetic abstract structures. The tone shifts across an emotional arc—from joyful childlike exploration to introspective dissolution and serene minimalism. Perfect for surreal storytelling, abstract landscapes, dream maps, poetic illustration, and conceptual design. ✨
